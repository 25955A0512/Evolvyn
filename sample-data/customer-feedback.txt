SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA — NOT A REAL COMPANY

Business: Northstar Tasks, a small project management SaaS.
Goal: improve paid conversion without harming retention.

Customer interview notes, demonstration sample:
- Participant A reported that the onboarding checklist was confusing and asked why a card was required before the first project could be created.
- Participant B reported that the activation screen froze after inviting a teammate.
- Participant C said the product was useful after onboarding, but getting to the first successful project took too long.
- Participant D wanted a clearer explanation of pricing limits before starting a trial.

Known limitations:
These four synthetic interviews do not establish population-level sentiment or causality. No controlled experiment has run. Funnel data is supplied separately in funnel.csv.

Suggested investigation:
Validate event definitions and interview real consenting users before considering a reversible onboarding experiment. Do not contact anyone, send campaigns or deploy changes based on this sample.
